Research

Three projects are underway, all of them open.

Current projects

  • Tuberculosis drug resistance

    An open, versioned resource linking genetic variants to drug resistance outcomes for newer and repurposed tuberculosis drugs.

  • Kazakh biomedical language

    The first open Kazakh biomedical text resource, with named entity recognition and terminology mapping. Nothing like it exists yet.

  • Central Asian medicinal plants

    A computable knowledge base of the chemistry and traditional use of medicinal plants from a region global databases barely cover.

Earlier work

  • Purification of Wastewater from Dye Production

    A school chemistry project on removing dye from textile wastewater, with a full write-up and a public audit of it coming.
