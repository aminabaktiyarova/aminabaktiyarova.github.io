How it works

  1. Book a first step

    An IELTS diagnostic consultation, an IELTS mock test analysis, or an admissions consultation, paid at booking.

  2. Get your notes and plan

    Written notes or a summary, with a plan, by the end of the next working day.

  3. Choose what comes next

    A 4-week IELTS package, admissions mentorship, single reviews, or your own preparation with the plan.

  4. Work from your portal

    Plan, deadlines, documents, scores and feedback in one shared page for you and your parents. It stays yours after we finish.

What to expect

Included

  • Written feedback on every marked task and document
  • One written instruction after every lesson, call and marked task
  • A plan updated every week
  • Replies by the end of the next working day
  • A shared portal, kept after we finish

Not included

  • A guaranteed band score or admission
  • Essays, letters or applications written for you
  • Submitting applications or using your logins
  • Legal or visa services
  • Paying fees, translations or apostille on your behalf

Hours and policies

Hours
Tuesday to Saturday, 13:00 to 21:00, Astana time (UTC+5). Messages sent outside these hours get a reply on the next working day.
Booking
Lessons and calls are booked at least 24 hours ahead. Rescheduling needs 6 hours' notice, and a session missed without notice counts as held.
Payment
Kaspi in tenge in Kazakhstan, Tribute in euros elsewhere. First steps and reviews are paid at booking, packages and mentorship before the period starts.
Pausing
Any service can be paused at any time. The remaining days of a paid period can be used within 4 weeks, and the portal stays open.
Refunds
A paid period is not refunded once it has started. If the first lesson or call of your first period shows a poor fit, you get the price back minus the hours already worked.
Your part
5 to 10 hours a week for mentorship, more for IELTS packages. Replies within 1 working day. Your essays and letters are written by you.
Privacy
Your information stays private. An example from your work appears in my content only if you agree to that example.
