## Long-term mentorship

The full journey, from diagnosis and strategy through program selection, preparation, essays, submission, results, visa and move, and the first weeks abroad. Works with all countries. 
Research mentorship, helping you find and complete a real research project for your portfolio, is included here, not priced separately. 
Weekly is 60 EUR plus Tribute fees, monthly is 230 EUR plus Tribute fees.

### The portal

A summary dashboard, university and program list with requirements and odds, an achievements database (olympiads, projects, volunteering, certificates, research), essay drafts with written feedback, a document tracker, an exam tracker, a finances table, a task list, and a meeting log. It's yours to keep after mentorship ends.

## SAT, AP, and A-Level preparation

| Format | Includes |
|---|---|
| Full support | 4 lessons/week (60 to 90 min), weekly full-length timed practice test, reviewed homework, portal. 300 EUR plus Tribute fees/month. |
| Standard | 2 lessons/week, practice test with review, portal. 150 EUR plus Tribute fees/month. |

The diagnostic consultation determines whether you need skill-based or subject-content tutoring, and packages are scoped per subject. Two AP subjects means two separate diagnostics and two separate subscriptions.

## For schools, centres, and companies

| Service | What it is |
|---|---|
| Curriculum audit | A structured review of an existing IELTS prep or TESOL course program, with a written findings and recommendations report. Starting at 250 EUR, scaled for full-program scope, quoted after a scoping call. |
| Curriculum design and coordination | Project-based support building or restructuring a course or program. Quoted per project after scoping. |
| Workshops and webinars | Single-session training for staff or students. 25 EUR per hour of session. |
| Partnerships | Referral, co-branded content, or ongoing collaboration with centres or private schools. Custom arrangement, discussed directly. |

## What is not included

- No guarantee of admission or a specific score.
- No ghostwriting. Essays and personal statements are brainstormed, drafted, edited, and reviewed together, but you write them and hold final responsibility.
- No legal or visa representation.
- No payment of third-party fees (translation, apostille, exam registration). These are your responsibility.
- Language exam prep (IELTS, SAT, and so on) is a separate service from admissions mentorship, not bundled by default.
