Research

Three projects are underway, all of them open.

Current projects

  • Bedaquiline and clofazimine resistance in tuberculosis

    An open, versioned resource measuring how mutations in Rv0678, pepQ and atpE shift bedaquiline and clofazimine MICs, built from the CRyPTIC Consortium dataset. Data tables and software released on 6 October 2026.

  • Kazakh biomedical language

    The first open Kazakh biomedical text resource, with named entity recognition and terminology mapping. Nothing like it exists yet.

  • Central Asian medicinal plants

    A computable knowledge base of the chemistry and traditional use of medicinal plants from a region global databases barely cover.

Earlier work

  • Purification of Wastewater from Dye Production

    A school chemistry project on removing dye from textile wastewater, and the public methodological review of it I wrote in 2026.
