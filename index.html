Amina Baktiyarova

Teaching, mentoring, and building open research projects of my own.

Currently based in Astana, working in Russian, English, and German.

Education

Graduated from Nazarbayev Intellectual School in 2022, on a scholarship. NIS schools admit by competitive entrance examination and run a heavier, more demanding programme than the standard national curriculum in Kazakhstan.

Lived and studied in Germany for four years, and now continuing my studies in the United States.

Research

I have worked in chemistry since 2018, starting with an experimental project in secondary school on removing dye from textile production wastewater, which was published and received a diploma at an international student research competition.

Three open research projects are underway now.

  • Tuberculosis drug resistance

    An open, versioned resource linking genetic variants to drug resistance outcomes for newer and repurposed tuberculosis drugs.

  • Kazakh biomedical language

    The first open Kazakh biomedical text resource, with named entity recognition and terminology mapping.

  • Central Asian medicinal plants

    A computable knowledge base of the chemistry and traditional use of medicinal plants from a region global databases barely cover.

Teaching and mentorship

My students come from Kazakhstan, Europe, Asia, and the Gulf.
